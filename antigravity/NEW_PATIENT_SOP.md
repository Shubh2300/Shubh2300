# 🏥 NEW PATIENT REFERRAL — WHAT TO DO
*Atlantic Pain & Wellness Institute — Intake SOP. The robot does the paperwork. You do the judgment.*

Referrals arrive by **email** (and fax). The automation runs every few minutes:
it reads the referral, makes the patient's Drive folder, pre-fills the forms,
and logs it. **Your job is to verify it and finish what a robot can't.**

---

## STEP 1 — Confirm the robot caught it *(2 min)*
- Open the referral email and look at its **labels**:
  - ✅ `processed` → robot handled it. Continue to Step 2.
  - ⚠️ `needs-review` → robot wasn't sure. **You** do the steps manually and clear the review.
  - ❌ No label after ~10 min → tell the intake lead; process manually.
- Search the patient's name in **Google Drive** → confirm the folder exists with the referral PDF + 3 pre-filled forms inside.

## STEP 2 — Verify the basics (check, don't trust)
- Name spelled correctly? **DOB matches the referral exactly?** Phone number present?
- Fix any error in the dashboard (open the patient → edit). A wrong DOB poisons everything downstream.
- Set the **case type: WC or MVA**. If you can't tell from the referral — **ask, don't guess.**

## STEP 3 — Claim it in the dashboard
- Top of the dashboard: set **"Working as: YOUR NAME."** All work is tracked by name.
- Find the patient in the pipeline. Status "— Not Set" → set it to **Pending**.
- Open the auto-created **Intake Review task** → assign it to yourself.

## STEP 4 — Collect the usual four documents
☐ Driver's license  ☐ Insurance card  ☐ Accident / incident report  ☐ Signed intake form
- Anything missing → mark **"Info Needed"** on the patient so the whole team can see it, then call the patient (or attorney's office).
- Email drafts contain **[FILL IN]** blanks on purpose. **Fill every blank before sending. Never send a guess.**

## STEP 5 — Benefits & legal (by case type)
- **WC:** verify claim #, carrier, adjuster name/phone, employer. Confirm authorization before booking procedures.
- **MVA/PIP:** verify PIP benefits. If an attorney is involved, get the **LOP or signed lien** before the surgical track moves.
- Write what you verified into the patient's **running notes**: carrier, who you spoke to, date.

## STEP 6 — Head injury? Special track
- Referral mentions **concussion / TBI / head injury** → confirm the patient is on the **Head Injury tab**, then follow the head-injury intern guide (Sway baseline, VOMS screen) before the first visit.

## STEP 7 — Schedule & confirm
- Book the appointment. **Call the patient to confirm** — and log the call note on their record.
- Booking confirmed → mark your task **Completed**. Done means *documented*, not just done.

## STEP 8 — Something looks wrong?
- Robot filed a vendor email as a patient? Duplicate folder? Wrong person?
  **Don't quietly delete anything.** Flag it to the intake lead, fix the record, and leave a note saying what happened.

---

### ⚡ THE THREE RULES
1. **Never invent data.** A blank beats a guess — write "—" or leave [FILL IN].
2. **Everything under your own name.** Set "Working as" before you touch anything.
3. **When unsure, ask.** Five minutes of asking beats a week of unwinding a mistake.

*Questions → intake lead. Dashboard is the source of truth — if it's not in there, it didn't happen.*

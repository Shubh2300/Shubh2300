# Vendor Data-Feed Request Emails

The durable way to keep the app's billing current (instead of fragile browser scraping)
is a **supported data export or API** from each billing vendor. You're a paying customer —
they can usually set this up. Copy/paste and send to your account rep at each.

---

## Email 1 — SIS Complete

**To:** your SIS Complete account manager / support
**Subject:** Data export / API access request — Atlantic Pain & Wellness Institute

Hi [Name],

We're building an internal operations dashboard for the practice and would like to pull our
billing data from SIS Complete in an automated, supported way rather than manually.

Could you let me know what options are available for our account:

1. **Scheduled export** (e.g., a nightly CSV delivered via SFTP or email) of our patients'
   billing — ideally including: patient name, DOB, date of service, CPT code(s),
   surgery-center status, payments/collected, outstanding balance, claim/EOB status, and
   payer/responsible party.
2. **API access** (REST or similar) to the same data, with documentation and credentials.

If there's a setup process, cost, or BAA/data-use agreement involved, please point me to it.
We just need a reliable, read-only feed of our own practice's data.

Thank you,
[Your name]
Atlantic Pain & Wellness Institute
[phone]

### Why this matters
A vendor feed doesn't break when their website UI changes, doesn't need anyone logged in,
and gives the *complete* dataset in one pass — so the dashboard can stay current
automatically. It's the real backbone for the "one-stop hub."
